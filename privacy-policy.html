MiladGeo Job Market

Privacy Policy

How information may be handled when you use our job platform.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

MiladGeo Job Market is a platform intended to connect job seekers and employers in surveying, geomatics, civil engineering, and related fields. This policy describes the types of information the website may process and how that information may be used. It should be read alongside the features and settings available on the website.

2. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: name and email address, along with information needed to authenticate your account.
  • Job listing information: job title, description, location, and contact details provided by a poster.
  • Resume information: professional title, education, experience, skills, and other details you choose to submit.
  • Technical information: limited information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and the website explicitly supports its safe handling.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, support account verification and password resets, manage job listings and resumes, send essential service notifications, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service.

4. Job Listings and Resumes

Depending on publication settings and website features, job listings or resume details may be visible to other users. Review your information before submitting or publishing it. Do not include private contact details in publicly visible content unless you intend to share them.

5. Email Messages

The service may use an email delivery provider to send transactional messages such as account verification, password-reset instructions, and important account notifications. Email addresses should be used for these purposes in line with the user's interaction with the service. The platform does not intend to use purchased email lists or send unsolicited marketing messages.

6. Storage and Service Providers

The website may use Supabase for authentication and database services, and a third-party email provider for message delivery. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. The exact services in use may change as the platform develops.

7. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information and prevent unauthorized access. However, no website, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and Requests

Information may be retained for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information through the contact method provided on the website, where available. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may depend on hosting, authentication, database, or email delivery services operated by third parties. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply to their processing of information. We encourage users to review those policies.

10. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for professional job-seeking and recruitment. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection would violate applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website's features and practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the contact information published on the MiladGeo Job Market website, if available. Before launching the service publicly, the operator should add a monitored contact email or another reliable contact method here.
